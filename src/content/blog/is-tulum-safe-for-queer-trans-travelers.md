## Is Tulum queer and trans friendly?

Tulum isn't a queer-centric town — there's no gayborhood, no single famous gay bar. But that's not the same as being unwelcoming. You'll see queer and gender-diverse people any time you walk out on the street. We're a regular part of the community and of daily life here, woven into the town rather than concentrated in one corner of it.

Because Tulum is so international, acceptance tends to come naturally. People from all over the world live and visit here, and that diversity does a lot of quiet work.
